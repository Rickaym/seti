# Changelog

## 1.1.0

Answers stay as asides. Seti stores each answered question with who asked it, and the mod draws it
inline in the agent's transcript at the time it was asked. The dock lists the agent's asides and
follows up the newest chat after a reload or a restart, with New chat to start another; the
eight-question limit and the forgetting after 30 idle minutes are gone. Delete removes one aside
and Clear removes them all. The EA gets `asides` and `clear`. Needs mod API 1.21.

## 1.0.0

The first version, bundled with Seti. The side chat moved out of Seti's core into this mod.
