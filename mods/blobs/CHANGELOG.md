# Changelog

## 1.3.0

Blobs are redrawn as one flat shape in the agent's colour with a simple face that reads at 16 px. Running blobs look happy, idle ones are asleep with a small z, finished ones smile, and agents that need you look up with a question or exclamation mark. The Style setting is gone, and a saved style is dropped.

## 1.2.0

Blobs are redrawn with shading, a highlight, eyes with catchlights, and lids and brows for each face. They sit inside the row on every screen instead of past its edge. New settings choose their size and style (Round, Squishy or Ghost), turn animation off, and pick where they show: the agent list, Needs you and grid panels.

## 1.1.0

Agent rows show the blob as a bigger bookmark on their left edge, in place of the colour bar and the status marker.

## 1.0.0

The first version, bundled with Seti.
