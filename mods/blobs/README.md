# Blobs

Draws each agent as a small flat blob in its colour, with a face that shows what it is doing. In the agent list the blob sits at the row's left edge in place of the colour bar, and its face stands in for the status marker. In paused grid panels, the grid's agent picker, the legend, the phone agent header and Home, the status marker itself becomes a small blob.

## Faces

- running: open eyes and a small smile; the blob bobs and blinks.
- asks you: wide eyes looking up, with a question mark.
- waits on you: wide eyes looking up, with an exclamation mark.
- idle: asleep, with closed eyes and a small z.
- complete: happy closed eyes and a smile.
- error: crossed eyes and a small frown.
- stopped and interrupted: a neutral face; a stopped blob also fades.

The marks beside the head show on agent rows, where there is room for them.

## Settings

- Size: Small, Medium or Large. Every size fits inside the row, so rows keep their height.
- Animation: on by default. Running blobs never move when your system asks for reduced motion.
- Agent list, Needs you and Grid panels: where blobs show. Where they are off, rows keep the colour bar and Seti's status shape, and titles stay lined up.

Screen readers read the same status names as without the mod, and the blob shows the marker's tooltip.

It starts off, because it changes the look of every agent row. Turn it off to bring back the colour bars and Seti's shapes at once.
