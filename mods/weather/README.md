# Weather

Draws today's sky across the app header: the sun's arc from dawn to dusk, the moon with its phase, and clouds, rain or snow where you are.

## Settings

- **Weather** draws the clouds and rain, looked up near you every ten minutes. Turn it off to keep only the sun and moon.
- **Units** names the temperature in Celsius or Fahrenheit.
- **Location** takes a latitude and longitude, such as 52.5, 13.4. Left empty, it uses your time zone's city.

## What it may do

It asks api.open-meteo.com for the forecast and keeps the last answer in Seti.
