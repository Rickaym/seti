# Changelog

## 1.2.0

{ and } step back and forward through your own messages in the transcript on screen, as Alt+[ and Alt+] do.

## 1.1.0

Terminals no longer trap the keyboard. Ctrl+\ then n (or Ctrl+n) leaves a terminal for its panel in normal mode, and moving onto a terminal with h, l or a number stops on its panel instead of typing into the shell; i or a goes back in. While Vim mode is on, a lone Ctrl+\ no longer reaches the shell: press it twice to send one.
